Sketch a house. Walk through it.

Draw rooms, doors, windows, stairs and gardens on a 2D plan, furnish them, then look around in 3D or walk through in first person. A 7 MB desktop app; your plans never leave your computer.

Features

Everything you need to plan a home, and nothing you don't.

No account, no subscription, no cloud. Open it, draw, and it saves as you go.

Rooms in seconds

Drag rectangles, click out polygons, or stamp one of 23 furnished room templates. Walls and shared walls are worked out for you.

Exact sizes

Type 3.5, 350cm, 4x3 or 11'3" while drawing. Snapping to grid, corners and walls; metric or imperial.

Doors, windows, stairs

Openings slide along walls. Stairs are parametric (straight, L, U or spiral) and connect your floors.

84 furniture & garden items

Search, pick colour variants, drag them in, rotate and scale. Bring your own .glb models.

Gardens & outdoor rooms

Grass, patio, path, driveway and pool zones; trees, hedges and outdoor furniture; open patios with no walls or roof.

Multiple floors

Stack floors, duplicate one onto the next, and see the floor below ghosted while you trace over it.

Tour

Three ways to look at your plan.

Press Tab to switch between them at any time.

A clean 2D plan

Draw on a grid with live dimensions, areas and room labels. Light and dark themes.

  • Undo everything, autosaved
  • Room areas and gross / net totals
  • Command palette with Ctrl K

Furnish it from the catalog

Every item has a real size and a thumbnail. Drop it in and it snaps to the floor, a wall or the ceiling.

  • Sofas, beds, kitchens, bathrooms, offices
  • Trees, hedges, flower beds, loungers
  • Colour variants per item

Then walk in through the front door

First-person mode with WASD and the mouse. Walls stop you, doors let you through, stairs take you up.

  • Hold Shift to run
  • Orbit the whole house in 3D
  • Export the model to Blender as GLB

Start from a template

23 furnished rooms, from a powder room to a master suite or a great room with dining. Save your own rooms as templates too.

Export

Take your plan anywhere.

Print it to scale, open it in CAD, price it in a spreadsheet, or share the project file.
